Privacy Policy

Matchoose · Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Matchoose mobile app ("Matchoose", "the app", "we") handles your information. The short version: your photos and closet data stay on your device. We do not collect them, and we do not send them anywhere.

1. Who we are

Matchoose is developed by mottech. You can contact us at s6801012610197@email.kmutnb.ac.th.

2. Information the app handles

Matchoose lets you build a digital closet. To do this, the app creates and stores the following on your device:

  • Photos of your clothes that you take with the camera, saved as images in the app's private storage.
  • Item details you enter: name, category, color, style, and whether the item is a favorite.
  • App settings, such as your preferred text size.

This information is stored locally in the app's private storage on your device. We do not have access to it.

3. Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect, upload, or share your photos or closet data.
  • We do not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location.
  • We do not use advertising, and we do not use third-party analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not sell your data or share it with third parties.

4. Permissions

Camera: used only so you can photograph your clothes inside the app. Photos are processed on your device. You can allow or revoke this permission at any time in your device settings; without it, you will not be able to add new items by taking a photo.

5. How outfit recommendations work

Outfit recommendations are generated on your device from the items in your closet, using simple matching rules based on style and color. No data is sent to a server to create them.

6. Your choices and deleting your data

  • You can delete any item (and its photo) inside the app at any time.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all data the app stored on your device.
  • Because your data stays on your device, it is not included in a backup that we control. Your device's own backup settings (for example, Android backup) may include app data, subject to your device settings.

7. Children's privacy

Matchoose does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Since the app does not transmit your data, no personal information from children is received by us.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above. If the app's data practices change in a meaningful way, we will update this policy before the change takes effect.
